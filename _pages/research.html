CFD-Based Control of Convection with Source Term(s).

Employing the control law for regulating convection can provide an optimal system for many sophisticated applications, such as MEMS fabrication, electronic component cooling, and drug manufacturing facilities. A computational approach is always advantageous in this regard for affordable trial and error. A more advanced version of this can be established by creating a digital twin for the application. Other improvements include the use of nonlinear controllers for the system, inserting multiple sensors in the domain, and acquiring precise measurements. Another aspect involves quantifying the uncertainty and using it during feedback. This would require advanced data analysis in real-time and statistical methods to capture the system's dynamics and enable precise control.

A related published work (Deb et al., 2023†) only touches upon the basic aspects. This can be leveraged for many advanced applications with the aforementioned improvements.
